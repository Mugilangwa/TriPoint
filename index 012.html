WELCOME TO TRIPOINT LIMITED

TRIPOINT LIMITED is a Tanzanian-registered private company limited by shares, incorporated under the Companies Act, Cap 212. We are a diversified service provider headquartered in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, dedicated to simplifying business for corporates, NGOs, government agencies, and individuals under one roof.

What We Do

We provide a wide range of integrated, reliable, and affordable solutions across key sectors:

  • Mobility

    Reliable fleet and transport solutions tailored for corporate and personal transit needs.

  • Tourism

    Showcasing the best of Tanzania with expertly managed tourism and travel experiences.

  • Logistics

    Efficient supply chain, freight, and cargo delivery services across the region.

  • Technology

    Innovative tech infrastructure and digital solutions designed to accelerate business growth.

  • Mining Support

    Professional operational and logistical backup services tailored to the mining sector.

  • Agriculture

    Modern agricultural support systems promoting sustainable food production and farming.

  • General Commerce

    Diversified trade, procurement, and commercial supply solutions under one roof.
